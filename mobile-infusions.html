Mobile Infusions

Same nurse-administered infusions, delivered to your home, office, Airbnb or event anywhere in Western North Carolina.

Book a visit Or call (828) 476-8851

The treatment, plainly

Mobile infusions are the same nurse-administered drips you’d receive at our Hendersonville Road clinic — brought to you. We bring sterile supplies, an IV pole, and the same care you’d get in-clinic. Travel is billed as a flat fee based on your location, in addition to the therapy.

Is this right for me?

Travellers, post-event recovery after weddings, conferences and races, people working from home, anyone with mobility limitations, and groups booking infusions together.
