Red Light Therapy

Full-body red and near-infrared light therapy for cellular recovery, skin health, and inflammation. Pairs beautifully with any infusion.

Book a visit Or call (828) 476-8851

The treatment, plainly

Our medical-grade red light therapy bed delivers wavelengths of red (660 nm) and near-infrared (850 nm) light that stimulate mitochondrial function, support collagen production, and reduce inflammation. Sessions are non-invasive, painless, and feel like a warm sunbath.

Is this right for me?

Anyone managing chronic inflammation, joint pain, slow wound healing, skin concerns, or athletic recovery. Also widely used for mood, sleep, and general cellular wellness.
