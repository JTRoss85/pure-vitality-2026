The treatment, plainly

Wellness injections deliver concentrated vitamins, amino acids and lipotropic compounds intramuscularly. Because they bypass the digestive system, your body absorbs nearly the full dose within minutes — and you’re back to your day in less than fifteen.

Is this right for me?

People who want a consistent energy or immune boost, anyone with absorption issues, and clients building a weekly or biweekly wellness routine. Every plan starts with a one-on-one consultation with our practitioner during your first visit — if this service isn’t the right fit, we’ll tell you and suggest one that is.
