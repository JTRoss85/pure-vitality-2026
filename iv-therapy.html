Book a visit Or call (828) 476-8851

The treatment, plainly

IV therapy delivers therapeutic doses of vitamins, minerals and specialty compounds directly into your bloodstream. Because it bypasses the digestive system, your body absorbs close to 100% of the nutrients — compared with 20–40% from oral supplements. Every drip is mixed and administered by a registered nurse who stays with you for the duration of your session.

Is this right for me?

Clients managing fatigue, chronic illness, dehydration, athletic recovery, immune support, or specialty conditions like chronic Lyme. Anyone seeking therapeutic concentrations of nutrients beyond what oral supplements can deliver. Every plan starts with a one-on-one consultation with our practitioner during your first visit.
