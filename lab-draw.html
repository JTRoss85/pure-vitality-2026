Lab Draw

Specialized lab draws performed in-house — including iron panels, G6PD testing, CBC, CMP, and hormone work. Results routed to your provider or our network.

Book a visit Or call (828) 476-8851

The treatment, plainly

We perform routine and specialty lab draws in our Hendersonville Road clinic. Samples are processed through our reference-lab network and results are returned directly to your provider — or we coordinate the next step in your care plan with you.

Is this right for me?

Clients preparing for high-dose vitamin C or chelation IV therapy, which require baseline labs. Anyone tracking hormones or chronic-condition markers, and patients whose physician has ordered a specific panel.

The menu

Options & pricing

A standard lab draw is $50, whichever panel you need. If your order calls for more than one panel or specimen box, each additional one is $25 on top of that initial draw. We always confirm your total with you before we start.
